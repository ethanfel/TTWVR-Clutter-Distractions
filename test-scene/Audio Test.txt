TTWVR Clutter Distractions 0.2.7 — Ghoul hearing test

Install this ZIP as a mod in your Windows TTWVR profile. It includes Clutter
Distractions 0.2.7 and the test room. Enable TTWVRClutterDistractions.esp
and TTWVRAudioLab.esp, with the audio lab after Clutter Distractions.
Use TTWVR 1.1.7, xNVSE and JIP LN. Restart the game.

Make a separate save in a quiet location before entering. Open the game
console, enter the following command, then close the console.

    coc TTWVRAudioLab

You arrive with both ghouls disabled. The switch on the near face of the
crates starts or stops the trial. The ghouls start about twice the normal indoor
detection distance from the switch. Loading a save resets the ghouls. If you tried the old
short room, load a save made before you entered it to get fresh clutter.

1. Flip the entrance switch. You can stand or walk around the first table
   and compare a gentle set-down, a thrown can and a tap with the tire iron.
   This end of the range is for listening to the objects yourself.
2. To test what the ghouls hear, follow the long hall to the second table,
   behind a solid row of crates. Stand back from them and lob a can over
   the top. The ghouls can move freely
   on the far side to investigate the landing. Listen for their footsteps.
3. Two folded traps at the entrance let you test the arming click. The
   normal Repair requirement applies (55 by default). An armed trap sits
   beyond the right side of the divider. Throw near it to try to lure
   a ghoul across it.

Load your pre-test save to return to your game. To remove the room, disable
TTWVRAudioLab.esp. If this ZIP supplied your only copy of Clutter Distractions,
keep the mod enabled for its DLL, meshes and main ESP.
