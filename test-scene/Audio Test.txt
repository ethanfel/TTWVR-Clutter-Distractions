TTWVR Clutter Distractions 0.2.7 — Ghoul hearing test

Install this ZIP as a mod in your Windows TTWVR profile. It includes Clutter
Distractions 0.2.7 and the test room. Enable TTWVRClutterDistractions.esp
and TTWVRAudioLab.esp, with the audio lab after Clutter Distractions.
Use TTWVR 1.1.7, xNVSE and JIP LN. Restart the game.

Make a separate save in a quiet location before entering. Open the game
console, enter the following command, then close the console.

    coc TTWVRAudioLab

The two ghouls start disabled. The switch on the left starts or stops
the trial. They start about twice the normal indoor detection distance
away, facing the far end. The center aisle is open from end to end.
If you played an earlier room, load a save from before your first visit.

1. Flip the switch. The first table has cans, bottles, mugs and a tire iron
   for comparing a gentle set-down, a throw and a tap.
2. Walk to the second table for a clear view of the ghouls. Throw a can
   down the aisle and watch whether they turn or move toward the landing.
   You can walk closer for a shorter throw. They can see and hear you there.
3. Test the arming click with the two folded traps at the entrance
   (Repair 55 by default). An armed trap lies downrange on the right.

Load your pre-test save to return to your game. To remove the room, disable
TTWVRAudioLab.esp. If this ZIP supplied your only copy of Clutter Distractions,
keep the mod enabled for its DLL, meshes and main ESP.
