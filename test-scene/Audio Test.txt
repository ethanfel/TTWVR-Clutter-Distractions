TTWVR Clutter Distractions 0.2.7 — Ghoul hearing test

Install this ZIP as a mod in your Windows TTWVR profile. It includes Clutter
Distractions 0.2.7 and the test room. Enable TTWVRClutterDistractions.esp
and TTWVRAudioLab.esp, with the audio lab after Clutter Distractions.
Use TTWVR 1.1.7, xNVSE and JIP LN. Restart the game.

Make a separate save in a quiet location before entering. Open the game
console, enter the following command, then close the console.

    coc TTWVRAudioLab

You arrive behind two tall crates. Both feral ghouls start disabled.
The small switch on the near face of the crates starts or stops the test.
Loading a save resets the ghouls. If you save here before testing, reloading
that save also restores the clutter and trap placements from that moment.

1. Stay behind the crates. The table on your left has loose metal cans, glass
   bottles, mugs and a tire iron. Try a gentle set-down and a hard throw with
   the ghouls off to hear the difference.
2. Activate the switch and remain behind cover. Throw a can past the left
   edge into the open lane. Listen for the landing and for ghoul movement.
   For a second comparison, hold a can and tap it with the equipped tire iron.
   Stepping out exposes you to normal sight detection.
3. Two folded traps are on the floor to your right. Pull one open with both
   hands to hear the latch. The normal Repair requirement applies (55 by
   default). A separate trap is already armed in the right lane. Lure a ghoul
   across it to hear the snap and impact from where the jaws are.

Load your pre-test save to return to your game. To remove the room, disable
TTWVRAudioLab.esp. If this ZIP supplied your only copy of Clutter Distractions,
keep the mod enabled for its DLL, meshes and main ESP.
